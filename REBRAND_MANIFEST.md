## How the app label actually works here (read before looking for app_name)

Upstream Moonlight has **no `app_name` string** and no `android:label` on the main
`<application>` element. The launcher/leanback label is generated by `resValue` entries in
`app/build.gradle` (`app_label` / `app_label_root`) and bound by the flavor manifests
(`app/src/nonRoot/AndroidManifest.xml` → `@string/app_label`,
`app/src/root/AndroidManifest.xml` → `@string/app_label_root`). The rebrand therefore
changes the label in `app/build.gradle` (below) — `strings.xml` is untouched in every locale
(verified: zero string names added or dropped anywhere).
